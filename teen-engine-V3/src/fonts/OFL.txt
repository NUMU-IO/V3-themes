Genova bundles two typefaces, both under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
which permits redistribution and self-hosting:

  Instrument Sans      — Copyright (c) Rodrigo Fuenzalida, Jordan Egstad
  IBM Plex Sans Arabic — Copyright (c) IBM Corp.

Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The .woff2 files here are the standard Google Fonts builds, subset to the
unicode ranges Genova actually renders (latin + latin-ext for the grotesk,
arabic + latin for the Arabic face). Regenerate them with
scratchpad/selfhost_fonts.py rather than editing by hand.
